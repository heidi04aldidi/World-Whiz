# WorldWhiz 🌍

Welcome to **WorldWhiz**! Your go-to destination for exploring the details of countries worldwide. With a simple search, you can get fascinating facts about any country, including its flag, capital, population, region, and live weather data. Explore stunning images related to the country with a click, thanks to our flip-card feature!

## Features ✨

- **Country Search**: Type in the name of any country to instantly view its details such as the flag, capital, population, region, and weather.
- **Weather Information**: Get live weather updates for the capital city of the country.
- **Country Glimpses**: Click on the card to flip it and explore beautiful images related to the country, fetched from Unsplash.
- **API Integration**: Uses **RestCountries API**, **Unsplash API**, and **OpenWeather API** to provide real-time country information.

## Technologies Used 🛠️

- **React** – Frontend library for building interactive UIs.
- **RestCountries API** – Get details about countries such as name, flag, capital, population, region, etc.
- **OpenWeather API** – Fetch real-time weather data for the country's capital city.
- **Unsplash API** – Display beautiful photos related to the country.
- **CSS** – For styling with flip-card animations and a sleek user interface.
